# 35. TTS

TTS means text-to-speech. It converts written text into spoken audio.

TTS is used in voice assistants, accessibility tools, navigation, education, and customer support.

## Voice Quality

Good TTS should sound natural, clear, and appropriate for the product. Voice choice affects user trust and comfort.

## Latency

Interactive voice systems need low latency. If the system waits too long before speaking, the conversation feels broken.

Streaming audio generation can improve responsiveness.

## Text Preparation

The input text may need cleanup before synthesis. Numbers, abbreviations, URLs, code snippets, and punctuation can affect speech.

For example, "Dr." may mean "doctor" or "drive" depending on context.

## Safety And Controls

Voice systems can be misused. Applications may need controls for impersonation, harmful content, or unauthorized voice cloning.

## Key Ideas

- TTS converts text into speech.
- Voice quality and latency both matter.
- Text normalization improves output.
- Voice features need safety controls.

## Developer Checklist

- Test generated speech with real product text.
- Measure time to first audio.
- Normalize text before synthesis.
- Follow consent and safety rules for custom voices.
